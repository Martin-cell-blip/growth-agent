§1 · How it works

Fix the mechanical problems yourself. Spend the official round on economics.

Paste your commentary

Your draft plus the news article it responds to. Nothing to install for the demo.

Get criterion-referenced checks

Word limit, diagram, terminology, key concepts, article grounding, evaluation angles, claim-evidence gaps — mapped to criteria A–E, in seconds.

Revise, re-run, repeat

Every round is logged — word count, similarity to your last draft, band trajectory — exportable as a process report.

§2 · The contract

It will never write it for you.

That's not a policy promise — it's enforced in code. If the AI breaks a rule, its output is rejected before you see it.

R·01

Every quote must exist verbatim in your draft — no invented sentences, ever.

R·02

Feedback comes as questions, not rewrites — every finding ends with a question you answer yourself.

R·03

Every finding is anchored to evidence — a named check result or a line from your own text. Unanchored advice is rejected.

R·04

AI never changes the mechanical results — deterministic checks run first and stand as computed.

§3 · The questions it asks

One question, four workflows: which step of your reasoning is still unproven?

“Whose losses, or what time frame, would weaken your judgement if taken seriously?”

“What is the step-by-step mechanism from the policy to the outcome you assert?”

evaluation chain · mechanism step
